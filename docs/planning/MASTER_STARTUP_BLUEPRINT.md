## Unschool Startup – Master Summary Blueprint

This document captures the **final summary** of the unschool startup vision and model.  
Use it as the single reference for high-level positioning, product, and non‑negotiables.

---

### 🧠 1. What the Startup Is

- A learn-by-doing, curiosity-first education model for children aged 6–18 years.
- No online classes, no teaching sessions, no tutors.
- Only physical activity materials delivered to the child every month.
- Children explore activities independently, without adult instruction.
- Philosophy: **"Learning without teaching" → self-driven discovery.**

---

### 📦 2. What the Product Is

- A monthly curiosity kit (box) fully personalized based on:
  - Child’s interests (music, dance, science, art, robotics, etc.)
  - Age group (6–10, 11–14, 15–18)
  - Skill-development goals (creativity, logic, cultural skills, physical skills, etc.)
- Each box contains:
  - 5–7 hands-on activities
  - High-quality materials
  - Guides/instructions designed for self-learning
  - Free pick-up & drop delivery system
  - Reuse of materials where possible (saves cost, eco-friendly)

---

### 🎯 3. Target Customer

- Parents who believe:
  - Traditional schooling alone is not enough.
  - Children learn best by doing, exploring, and creating.
  - Their child should grow holistically, not just academically.
- Long-term, value-aligned parents — **NOT** short-term or bargain seekers.
- You do **NOT** want to attract the mass audience who only seeks cheap prices.

---

### 💰 4. Pricing Philosophy

- Goal: **Sustainable revenue from Day 1**, not loss-leaders.
- Subscription-only model (no one-time purchases).
- Pricing must be:
  - Simple
  - Unconfusing
  - Emotionally appealing
  - Fair even if different materials cost different levels

---

### 💳 5. Final Subscription Model (Chosen Strategy)

**One Core Plan → Three Duration Options**

Same plan, same quality, same personalization.

| Plan Name     | Duration | Price  | Positioning              |
|--------------|----------|--------|--------------------------|
| Step Starter | 1 month  | ₹999   | Try the first box        |
| Path Explorer| 3 months | ₹2,699 | Habit-building, discounted|
| Life Learner | 6 months | ₹4,999 | Long-term growth, best value |

**Why this model is chosen:**

- Zero customer confusion
- Easier marketing & Instagram messaging
- One system, not multiple variants
- Handles the “custom materials cost difference” issue by averaging cost across all interests
- Encourages long-term commitment
- Builds the “movement” feel

---

### 🧩 6. Customization Strategy (Important Constraint Solved)

**Problem:**  
Some interests (music, robotics) cost more; others (dance, logic puzzles) cost less.

**Solution:**

- Use home kits + reusable items to offset costs.
- Standardize internal material cost target (₹300–₹600 per box).
- Personalize activities, **not** the price.
- Long-term subscriptions let higher/lower cost months balance each other.
- Keep pricing the same for all interests (no tier confusion).

---

### 📱 7. Social Media & Growth Strategy

- Only focus on **Instagram** (reels + posts).
  - No YouTube, no website blogs, no multi-platform chaos.
- Spend money on boosting IG posts early on.

**Content strategy:**

- Short storytelling reels about how children learn by themselves
- Behind-the-scenes of making curiosity boxes
- Transformation videos (child exploring independently)
- Box-unboxing reels
- Parent testimonials
- Founder storytelling (“Why I built this unschool movement”)

**CTA (every time):**

> “DM **UNSCHOOL** to join the movement — monthly personalized curiosity kits.”

### 🧱 11. Non‑Negotiables

The model must **always** follow these:

- No online classes.
- No video teaching.
- No multiple pricing tiers.
- No niche focus — **ALL interests allowed.**
- Only physical experiential kits.
- Only Instagram as marketing channel.
- Only one subscription model (duration-based).

---

### 🚀 12. Vision

- Build a **movement of self-driven learners**.
- Become India’s first **“teaching-free learning system.”**
- Create a long-term, trust-based relationship with aligned parents.
